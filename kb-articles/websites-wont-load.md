# Websites won't load on your PC

**For:** Users · **Applies to:** Windows 10/11

## Symptoms
- Web pages don't open.
- You see *"Can't reach this page"* or *"No internet"*.

## Cause
Usually a problem with your computer's connection. Sometimes the problem is wider and affects other people too.

## Steps
1. **Check you're connected.** Click the **Wi-Fi icon** at the bottom right of the screen. If you use a cable, check both ends are plugged in.
2. **Try a different website.** If some open and others don't, the problem may be that one site. Try it again later.
3. **Try the same website on your phone**, with Wi-Fi **switched off** so it uses mobile data. If it doesn't work there either, the site itself may be down.
4. **Ask a colleague nearby** whether their internet works. If it's down for them too, contact IT: the problem is probably not your computer.
5. **Close your web browser completely** and open it again.
6. **Restart your computer:** **Start → Power → Restart**, not Shut down. (On Windows, Restart clears more than Shut down does.)

## When to contact IT
If none of these steps work, contact IT through the IT portal or by phone, and include:
- the exact error message
- a screenshot
- when it started
- whether colleagues are affected
- which of these steps you've already tried
